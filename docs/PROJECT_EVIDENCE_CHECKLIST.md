# Project evidence checklist

Use this as an intake list before strengthening any outcome claim. “Not measured” is
better than an estimated or reconstructed result.

For every new claim, record:

- The source or artifact.
- The date and time period.
- The sample size and who participated.
- Ayse’s exact role and, for team projects, the ownership split.
- A link, screenshot, file, or written confirmation that can be cited.
- Permission to publish names, quotes, internal screens, and sensitive data.
